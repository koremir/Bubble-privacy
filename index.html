Privacy Policy

Pop Million: Bubble Wrap
Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Pop Million: Bubble Wrap. Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile game ("the Game") handles information, including data processed through advertising services and in-app purchases.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required, it will be requested separately.

Privacy at a Glance

Pop Million is designed primarily as an offline game. You do not need to create an account to play.

Optional rewarded advertisements and Google Play purchases require an internet connection.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores certain information locally to maintain your gameplay experience.

This information may include:

  • Bubble popping progress and statistics
  • Remaining bubble counter
  • Virtual currency balances
  • Daily rewards and mission progress
  • Unlocked bubble skins and cosmetic items
  • Collection fragments and unlocked pictures
  • Purchased items and local entitlement records
  • Audio, vibration, and visual preferences

The Game does not require a user account and does not currently provide its own cloud synchronization service.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing application storage may permanently erase locally stored progress and consumable virtual currency.

3. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements.

You may choose to watch advertisements to receive additional virtual currency, collection fragments, or other in-game rewards.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network data
  • Device model and operating system
  • Application and device identifiers
  • Advertisement interactions
  • Advertising performance and diagnostic data

This information may be used to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Where required, advertising consent is managed through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Users may access applicable advertising privacy choices through the Game's privacy settings.

For more information: Google Privacy Policy

4. In-App Purchases

Pop Million offers optional in-app purchases such as:

  • Virtual coin packages
  • Bubble skins and cosmetic items
  • Visual and sound effect packages
  • Collection unlocks
  • Other optional digital content

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing using Unity In-App Purchasing.

We do not directly collect or store your complete payment card details. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

Purchase-related information, including product identifiers, purchase tokens, transaction status, and technical identifiers, may be processed to deliver purchases, validate transactions, and prevent duplicate rewards.

Eligible permanent purchases may be restored through Google Play. Consumable virtual currency may not be recoverable after local application data is deleted.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

5. Unity and Technical Information

The Game is developed using the Unity engine.

Depending on the Unity services and third-party software components enabled in the released application, technical information may be processed.

This may include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application version
  • Technical installation identifiers
  • Crash and error information
  • Performance and diagnostic information

For additional information: Unity Privacy Policy

6. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the Game and its integrated services may be used to:

  • Save and restore gameplay progress
  • Manage virtual items and rewards
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Provide optional rewarded advertising
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Maintain application functionality
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Manage applicable privacy preferences

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell locally stored gameplay progress to advertisers.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary for operating specific Game features, including:

  • Google AdMob — optional advertising
  • Google Play — payments and purchases
  • Unity — game engine and enabled services

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

Data may be processed in countries other than your country of residence.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information generally remains on your device until you clear the Game's data or uninstall the application.

Third-party services may retain data according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

You can remove locally stored Game data through your device's application settings.

Please note that deleting application data may permanently remove gameplay progress and consumable virtual items.

For privacy requests involving information under our control, please contact us using the email address below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Game does not require players to submit their name, email address, or other personal details to play.

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Advertising practices and privacy settings will be configured in accordance with the applicable age groups, laws, and Google Play policies for the Game.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information relating to a child, please contact us.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Game.

However, no digital storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information.

Where available, you can manage advertising consent through the Game's privacy settings.

You can also manage advertising preferences through your Google account and device settings.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Game, third-party services, or applicable laws.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us
